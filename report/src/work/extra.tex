As Dr Shiva Ram Reddy was also in another research project where the experiments
were conducted by Research Assistant Mehjabin Shirin, I participated in that one
as a subject. It is a a collaborative research study conducted by IIT Madras and
the Davos Alzheimer's initiative, in association with the University of
Hyderabad. The project focuses on identifying early biomarkers of Alzheimer's
disease using MoCA, speech perception, smell perception, and face perception
tests.
