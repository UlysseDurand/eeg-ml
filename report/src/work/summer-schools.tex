\subsubsection{Digital Humanities and NLP}

A Faculty Development Programme on Digital Humanities and NLP happened at
Mahindra University where Dr. Nidhi Goyal was a lecturer.  People who attended
the lab came from many subjects, linguistics, neuroscience, education and spoke
many indic languages.

The lab happened after a NLP course, going through many concepts of linguistics,
but the lab was about using Python so the attendee could practice with the
concepts they just learnt. The attendee did not necessary were comfortable with
Python so Gaspard Lefort and I acted as teaching assistants, we had to help them
individually during the lab. The lab was about using a Python Indic NLP Library
so they could apply the course to their mothertongue. This was for a total of 4
lab hours.
